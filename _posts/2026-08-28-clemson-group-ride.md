In fall 2026, a couple of us were talking amongst ourselves about the state of bike infrastructure in and around Clemson and identified a lack of community and interaction between various forms of bike and related recreation in and around Clemson. A few of us decided on getting a casual weekly community group ride going in Clemson.

After some thoughts on availability and other constraints, we are happy to announce two group rides. A weekly shorter ride on **Thursday afternoon ** meeting at 6 PM inside Clemson University by the Reflection Pond, and a second longer, more challenging and longer ride during the weekend, biweekly on Saturday for more experienced riders.

Please keep in touch with Our [instagram](https://www.instagram.com/bikeclemson) for further updates
